uSiS Technologies is a software development and IT consulting company headquartered in Coimbatore, India, established in 2007. The company specializes in digital transformation through custom software development, enterprise applications, AI and data intelligence, ERP and HR solutions, e-commerce platforms, mobile and web applications, startup product engineering, and IT staff augmentation. It serves industries including retail, healthcare, manufacturing, banking, hospitality, HR, real estate, events, and waste management. Its technology stack includes .NET, React, Angular, Node.js, PHP, Flutter, Python, MongoDB, MySQL, Azure AI, ERPNext, and Frappe HR. The company focuses on agile development, customer-centric solutions, scalable architectures, and long-term technology partnerships, offering consulting, implementation, integration, maintenance, and managed services.
